지문 흐름 (Flow)

주제 일본 전통 가옥의 특징 — 추위·비·불에 대비한 구조와 생활 방식

흐름문장핵심 내용연결어·키워드
① 도입S1일본 전통 가옥에서 산다면? (질문)traditional
② 현관S2겐칸에서 신발을 벗는다If ~, would
③ 바닥S3–4의자 대신 다다미 위에 앉는다Instead (대조)
④ 방·문S5–6쇼지로 방을 나누고, 모임 땐 떼어 큰 방으로portable, removed
⑤ 겨울S7–8코타츠에서 몸을 따뜻하게When cold
⑥ 여름(비)S9–10홍수 대비 바닥을 띄움to keep ~ dry
⑦ 지붕S11–13경사 지붕 + 방수·내화 카와라 기와also, not only ~ but also

구조: 질문 → 공간 순서(현관 → 방 안) → 계절 순서(겨울 → 여름) → 집 바깥(지붕)

원문

1What would it be like to live in a traditional Japanese house? 2If you lived there, you would have to take off your shoes in the genkan, an entrance hall, before entering the house. 3In the house, many Japanese do not use chairs. 4Instead, they sit directly on tatami mats that are made from woven grass. 5Rooms are divided by shoji, portable sliding doors that are made from wood and thin paper. 6For large gatherings, these doors can be removed to create one large room. 7When the weather gets cold, people prefer to sit at a kotatsu, a heated table, and put their legs in it. 8Keeping their body warm, Japanese have meals, drink tea, or read books at the table. 9In summer, heavy rain can cause flooding. 10The floor of the house is raised a little off the ground to keep the living area dry and safe from flooding. 11The house also has sloped roofs because they keep rainwater flowing off easily. 12The roofs are covered with kawara tiles. 13The strong kawara tiles are not only water-resistant but also fireproof.

문장단위 분석

S01POINT
  • 가주어 it – 진주어 to부정사
  • What is it like ~? (~은 어떠한가?)
① 도입
핵심어traditional 전통적인

What wouldV it가S beV like to live in a traditional1 Japanese house진S?

직독직해

/What would it be like어떨까
/to live사는 것은
/in a traditional Japanese house?일본 전통 가옥에서

해석 일본 전통 가옥에서 사는 것은 어떨까?

결국 이 얘기 ‘일본 전통 집에서 살면 어떨까?’ 하고 질문으로 글을 시작해요.

문법
  • it = to live in a traditional Japanese house (가주어 – 진주어)
  • like는 전치사 ‘~와 같은’ → What is it like? = 어떠한가? (동사 like 아님)
  • would = 상상·가정. S2의 가정법 과거로 이어짐
단어 1traditional 전통적인 (tradition 전통)
S02POINT
  • 가정법 과거 If + 과거, would + 동사원형
  • 동격 콤마 / before + 동명사

결국 이 얘기 일본 집에 들어갈 때는 현관(겐칸)에서 신발부터 벗어요.

서술형 포인트 would have to take off ← 가정법 과거 주절: would + 동사원형
문법
  • 가정법 과거: If + 주어 + 과거동사(lived), 주어 + would + 동사원형 → 현재 사실의 반대
  • 직설법 전환: As you don't live there, you don't have to ~
  • would have to (O) / would must (X)
  • the genkan = an entrance hall (동격)
  • before + 동명사 entering / enter는 타동사 → enter into (X)
단어 1entrance hall 현관2enter 들어가다

문법

S05POINT

  • 동격 + 주격 관계대명사 that
