If you stayed in a hanok, you would sleep on the floor because there are no beds. Hanok houses are mostly built with natural materials such as wood, stone, straw, paper, and earth. These materials help you keep your skin clean. In the cold winter, the warm ondol floors heat your body. The doors in hanok are covered with thin paper. They help keep you cool in summer.

5.[5~7] 위 글에서 Pascal이 숙제하기를 어려워하는 이유는? [4점][8과]

Pascal was doing his math homework in his room. He was struggling with graphs.
"It's too hard to read and draw graphs. Why do I need these anyway? No one needs graphs in real life." He put down his pen and picked up his favorite book, Robin Hood. He decided to read himself to sleep. When he was about to open the book, he heard a voice. He looked up from the book to see who was talking. He couldn't believe his eyes. It was his dog, Manny, who was talking!
"Close your eyes and repeat after me. Cogito ergo sum," said Manny.
"You can talk?"
"Just repeat! Cogito ergo sum."
Pascal closed his eyes and repeated the words. Suddenly, he heard men shouting. When he opened his eyes, he saw soldiers on horses. They were chasing a man with arrows in his hand. The man saw Pascal and shouted.
"It's too dangerous for you to stand there. Come on." The man pulled Pascal onto his horse and rode into the woods.
When they arrived at a house, the man stopped and got off his horse.
"Hello, my name is Robin Hood."
"Wow! Are you the Robin Hood from the book?"
"No, I'm the Robin Hood of Sherwood Forest. Who are you and why are you here?"

  1. ①He didn't like writing essays.
  2. ②He forgot to bring his math book.
  3. ③He wanted to walk his dog instead.
  4. ④He didn't understand the book because he was not good at English grammar.
  5. ⑤He found graphs hard to read and difficult to draw, and doubted their practical use.

13 ③ 이유 ▶ Emily (B) 안됐다, 얼굴 근육이 피곤하겠다 → Jimmy (D) 무슨 뜻이야? → Emily (C) 화난 표정엔 근육이 많이 필요해 → Jimmy (A) 알겠다 → Emily That's right.

14 ① 이유 ▶ Claire (B) 위험한 동물이 있어 → Jinho (D) 알아, 하지만 모험 → Claire (A) 정글에 살면 뭘 할 거야? → Jinho (E) 탐험 → Claire (C) 어디서 자? 동굴? → Jinho No, tree house

15 ① 이유 ▶ would build ~ and (would) live → 조동사 뒤라 동사원형 live. lived ❌
포인트 ▶ 가정법 과거: If + 주어 + 과거, 주어 + would/could + 동사원형

16 ④ 이유 ▶ 제목은 'Gaudí and Spain' → 여러 나라가 아니라 스페인
오답 ▶ ① I want to visit Spain ② That is the church Antoni Gaudí designed, isn't it? ③ inspired by nature ⑤ I love it!

17 ① 이유 ▶ leave + 목적어 + 형용사 보어: 문이 '닫힌' 상태 → closed. close ❌
오답 ▶ ② make + 목적어 + worried ③ made + easier ④ makes + healthier ⑤ keep + safe → 모두 바름

18 ① 이유 ▶ too ~ to … 너무 ~해서 …할 수 없다 ✔ (주어 This problem이 solve의 목적어라 it을 쓰지 않음)
오답 ▶ ② everyone은 단수 → agrees ③ pets 복수 → are ④ 친구와 놀다 → to play with ⑤ so ~ that 절에는 목적어 필요 → buy it

19 ② 이유 ▶ the water ~ rises and leave → 주어 the water(단수)에 맞춰 leaves
오답 ▶ ⓐ 조건문 ⓒ 간접의문문 how and why + 주어 + 동사 ⓓ to부정사(목적) ⓔ 가주어 it ~ for them to → 모두 바름

20 ④ 이유 ▶ 늪지에 집을 짓기 어려워 나무 기둥을 박았다 → 기둥이 건물을 받치고 도시를 서 있게 한다 (본문: support Venice to this day)
오답 ▶ ① 다리 ② 아름다움 ③ 파도 ⑤ 배의 이동

21 ④ 이유 ▶ Not everyone = 모두가 ~인 것은 아니다(부분 부정) → 피울 수 있는 사람도 있음
오답 ▶ ①②③⑤ 아무도 피울 수 없다(전체 부정)
포인트 ▶ no one / nobody = 전체 부정, not everyone / not all = 부분 부정

22 ④ 이유 ▶ protect each other by living together → (A) 안전을 지키다(ensure safety) / work together, share many things → (B) 끈끈한 사회적 유대를 쌓다(build strong social bonds)
오답 ▶ ① 무역·부 ② 소음·사생활 ③ 돈·자유 ⑤ 자연·환경

23 ⑤ 이유 ▶ 지붕 위에서 편하게 잔다 → 지붕은 뾰족한(pointy)이 아니라 평평한(flat) 지붕
오답 ▶ ⓐ 숨겨진 ⓑ 반갑지 않은 ⓒ 막다 ⓓ 따뜻한 → 모두 자연스러움

24 ③ 이유 ▶ 가리키는 말이 graphs(복수) → it이 아니라 them
오답 ▶ ⓐ more ~ than ⓑ 관계대명사 what(~하는 것) ⓓ 지각동사 saw + 목적어 + -ing ⓔ say to oneself 혼잣말하다 → 모두 바름

25 ② 이유 ▶ too ~ for 의미상 주어 to … : 너무 ~해서 (누가) …할 수 없다 → for Jane to wear ✔
오답 ▶ ① Jane(의미상 주어)이 빠짐 ③④ 뜻이 반대 ⑤ Jane이 아니라 아무도(no one)
