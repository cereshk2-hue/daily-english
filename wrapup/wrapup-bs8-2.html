범위: 8과 문법 — not only A but also B(= B as well as A, 수 일치·병렬), 간접의문문(의문사+주어+동사, if/whether, think류 동사). 교재 문제를 바탕으로 문장을 바꾸고 더 어렵게 만들었어요. 문장을 끝까지 읽고 고르세요.

1.다음 문장과 의미가 같은 것은?[not only ~ but also]

Girls as well as boys enjoy the game.

  1. ①Not only boys but also girls enjoy the game.
  2. ②Only boys enjoy the game.
  3. ③Not girls but boys enjoy the game.
  4. ④Neither boys nor girls enjoy the game.
  5. ⑤Boys enjoy the game, but girls don't.
2.두 문장을 한 문장으로 바꿀 때 (A), (B)에 들어갈 말로 알맞은 것은?[not only ~ but also]

Mark speaks Spanish. He also speaks Italian.
→ Mark speaks not (A) Spanish (B) also Italian.

(A)(B)
①onlyand
②butonly
③justand
④onlybut
⑤alsobut
3.빈칸에 들어갈 말을 순서대로 바르게 짝지은 것은?[not only ~ but also]

• Both Ann and Jake coming to the party.
• Either you or I wrong.
• Kevin as well as his brothers tall.

  1. ①is − am − are
  2. ②are − am − is
  3. ③are − are − is
  4. ④are − am − are
  5. ⑤is − is − is
4.다음 중 어법상 어색한 문장은?[not only ~ but also]
  1. ①He gave up not only coffee but also soda.
  2. ②Not only she but also I am ready.
  3. ③Amy is not only pretty but also wise.
  4. ④My brother as well as my parents loves pizza.
  5. ⑤Not only the teacher but also the students was surprised.
5.다음 우리말을 바르게 영작한 것은?[not only ~ but also]

너는 책에서뿐만 아니라 사람들에게서도 많이 배울 수 있다.

  1. ①You can learn a lot not from books but from people.
  2. ②You can learn a lot either from books or from people.
  3. ③You can learn a lot from books as well as from people.
  4. ④You can learn a lot neither from books nor from people.
  5. ⑤You can learn a lot not only from books but also from people.
6.짝지어진 두 문장의 의미가 서로 다른 것은?[not only ~ but also]
  1. ①She is smart, and she is funny, too.
    = She is not only smart but also funny.
  2. ②He was a good singer as well as a good dancer.
    = He was not a good dancer but a good singer.
  3. ③Tea is loved by adults as well as children.
    = Tea is loved not only by children but also by adults.
  4. ④Not only Ted but also his dog was wet.
    = His dog as well as Ted was wet.
  5. ⑤The room is quiet as well as clean.
    = The room is not only clean but also quiet.
7.다음 중 어법상 옳은 문장은?[not only ~ but also]
  1. ①Not only did he win the game, but he also broke the record.
  2. ②Not only he won the game, but he also broke the record.
  3. ③Not only did he won the game, but he also broke the record.
  4. ④Not only won he the game, but he also broke the record.
  5. ⑤Not only does he win the game yesterday, but he also broke the record.
8.다음 문장을 간접의문문으로 바꿀 때 빈칸에 알맞은 것은?[간접의문문]

He said to me, "Are you hungry?"
→ He asked me .

  1. ①if you were hungry
  2. ②if was I hungry
  3. ③whether am I hungry
  4. ④if I was hungry
  5. ⑤that I was hungry

17.(A), (B), (C)에서 어법에 맞는 것을 바르게 짝지은 것은?[간접의문문]

• I have no idea how (A) [ they got / did they get ] there.
• Do you know who (B) [ broke / did break ] the vase?
• Do you know where (C) [ he is / is he ] from?

  1. ①did they get − broke − he is
  2. ②they got − did break − he is
  3. ③they got − broke − is he
  4. ④they got − broke − he is
  5. ⑤did they get − did break − is he

1 ① 이유 ▶ B as well as A = not only A but also B → 소년들뿐만 아니라 소녀들도
오답 ▶ ② 소년들만 ③ 소녀가 아니라 소년 ④ 둘 다 아님 ⑤ 소녀는 안 즐김

2 ④ 이유 ▶ not only A but also B

3 ② 이유 ▶ both A and B → 복수 are / either A or B → B(I) am / B as well as A → 앞(Kevin) is
포인트 ▶ not only A but also B · either A or B · neither A nor B → 동사는 B에 / B as well as A → 동사는 B(앞)에 / both A and B → 복수

4 ⑤ 이유 ▶ 동사는 B(the students, 복수)에 → were
오답 ▶ ① 명사끼리 ✔ ② B(I) → am ✔ ③ 형용사끼리 ✔ ④ 앞(My brother) → loves ✔

5 ⑤ 이유 ▶ 책뿐만 아니라(A) 사람들에게서도(B) → not only from books but also from people
오답 ▶ ① 책이 아니라 사람 ② 둘 중 하나 ③ B as well as A → '사람들뿐만 아니라 책에서도'(A·B가 바뀜) ④ 둘 다 아님

6 ② 이유 ▶ not A but B = A가 아니라 B → 둘 다라는 as well as와 뜻이 다름
오답 ▶ ①·③·④·⑤ not only A but also B = B as well as A

7 ① 이유 ▶ Not only가 문장 맨 앞이면 도치: Not only + did + 주어 + 동사원형
오답 ▶ ② 도치 안 함 ③ did 뒤에는 원형 win ④ 일반동사는 did를 써서 도치 ⑤ yesterday(과거) → did

8 ④ 이유 ▶ 의문사 없음 → if + 주어 + 동사. you는 나(I)로, 과거(asked)에 맞춰 was
오답 ▶ ① 인칭 ②·③ 어순 ⑤ that ❌

9 ③ 이유 ▶ last weekend(과거) + 의문사 + 주어 + 동사 → what Jenny did
오답 ▶ ①·⑤ 어순 ②·④ 시제

10 ⑤ 이유 ▶ how old + 주어 + 동사 → how old your brother is
오답 ▶ ①·②·③·④ 어순 ✔ (who가 주어면 바로 동사 owns)
포인트 ▶ 간접의문문 = 의문사 + 주어 + 동사 (do/does/did는 빼고 시제는 동사에)

11 ③ 이유 ▶ think → 의문사 맨 앞 + do you think + 주어 + 동사(과거)
오답 ▶ ① 의문사가 뒤 ② did 그대로 ④ 둘 다 ❌ ⑤ 샀다 → 과거
포인트 ▶ think · believe · suppose · guess · imagine 이면 의문사를 맨 앞으로: 의문사 + do you think + 주어 + 동사

12 ④ 이유 ▶ 의문사 what이 주어 → what + 동사(made)
오답 ▶ ① did ❌ ② 그가 무엇을 화나게? ③ 목적격 him이 주어 자리 ❌ ⑤ tell은 think류가 아님

13 ① 이유 ▶ 만약 ~라면(조건)
오답 ▶ ②·③·④·⑤ ~인지 아닌지(간접의문문, 목적어 역할)

14 ② 이유 ▶ 뒤가 완전한 문장 + '~인지' → if(whether)
오답 ▶ ①·③·④·⑤ have·did·bought·means의 목적어가 빠짐 → what

15 ③ 이유 ▶ 옳은 것 (a)·(c)·(e) 3개
오답 ▶ (b) what time it is (d) if he is busy (f) Where do you think she went?
포인트 ▶ think · believe · suppose · guess · imagine 이면 의문사를 맨 앞으로: 의문사 + do you think + 주어 + 동사

16 ⑤ 이유 ▶ 언제 자는지 → when + 주어 + 동사
오답 ▶ ①·③ 어순 ② 자는지 아닌지 → 대답과 맞지 않음 ④ tell은 think류가 아님

17 ④ 이유 ▶ (A) 주어 + 동사 (B) 의문사가 주어 → 바로 동사 (C) 주어 + 동사
포인트 ▶ 간접의문문 = 의문사 + 주어 + 동사 (do/does/did는 빼고 시제는 동사에)

18 ② 이유 ▶ 동명사끼리 나란히 ✔
오답 ▶ ① cooking과 나란히 → baking ③ reads와 나란히 → writes ④ 형용사 → comfortable ⑤ 명사(Paris)와 동사구는 나란히 쓸 수 없음 → not only Paris but also Rome

19 ③ 이유 ▶ 민호뿐만 아니라 친구들도 = His friends as well as Minho
오답 ▶ ① 둘 다 아님 ② 둘 중 하나 ④ 민호가 아니라 친구들 ⑤ 민호만

20 ① 이유 ▶ believe → 의문사를 맨 앞으로: Who do you believe will win?
오답 ▶ ②·⑤ 의문사 맨 앞 ✔ ③ if + 주어 + 동사 ✔ ④ 의문사 주어 + 동사 ✔
포인트 ▶ think · believe · suppose · guess · imagine 이면 의문사를 맨 앞으로: 의문사 + do you think + 주어 + 동사
