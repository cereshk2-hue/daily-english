범위: 8과 문법 — not only A but also B(= B as well as A, 수 일치·병렬), 간접의문문(의문사+주어+동사, if/whether, think류 동사). 교재 문제를 바탕으로 문장을 바꾸고 더 어렵게 만들었어요. 문장을 끝까지 읽고 고르세요.

1.(A), (B), (C)에 들어갈 말을 바르게 짝지은 것은?[not only ~ but also]

• Not only the boys but also the girl (A) happy.
• The girl as well as the boys (B) happy.
• Both the girl and the boys (C) happy.

  1. ①are − is − are
  2. ②is − is − are
  3. ③is − are − are
  4. ④are − are − are
  5. ⑤is − is − is
2.다음 중 어법상 옳은 문장은?[not only ~ but also]
  1. ①Not only she sings well, but she also dances well.
  2. ②Not only sings she well, but she also dances well.
  3. ③Not only does she sings well, but she also dances well.
  4. ④Not only did she sings well, but she also danced well.
  5. ⑤Not only does she sing well, but she also dances well.
3.두 문장을 한 문장으로 바르게 만든 것은?[not only ~ but also]
  1. ①Ken can swim. Ray can swim, too.
    → Not only Ken but also Ray can't swim.
  2. ②Sue is a nurse. She is also a singer.
    → Sue is not a nurse but a singer.
  3. ③This book is useful. It is also fun.
    → This book is fun as well as useful.
  4. ④Leo doesn't like milk. He doesn't like juice either.
    → Leo likes not only milk but also juice.
  5. ⑤Ann enjoys painting. She also enjoys singing.
    → Ann not only enjoys painting but also singing.
4.<보기>에서 어법상 옳은 문장을 모두 고른 것은?[not only ~ but also]

(a) Hana likes not only tea but also coffee.
(b) Not only I but also Mr. Kim want to be a teacher.
(c) Jinho is good at singing as well as dance.
(d) Not only the book but also the movie is fun.
(e) Sora as well as her cousins are good at tennis.
(f) Henry speaks not only English but Chinese.

  1. ①(a), (b), (d)
  2. ②(c), (d), (e)
  3. ③(b), (e), (f)
  4. ④(a), (d), (f)
  5. ⑤(a), (c), (f)
5.짝지어진 두 문장의 의미가 같은 것은?[not only ~ but also]
  1. ①He is good at math as well as science.
    = He is good at not only science but math.
  2. ②You can enjoy not only its taste but also its smell.
    = You can enjoy its smell well as its taste.
  3. ③Her eyes as well as her mouth smile.
    = Not only her eyes but also her mouth smiles.
  4. ④The plant is not only pretty but also useful.
    = The plant is not pretty but useful.
  5. ⑤Both Jim and Sue are absent.
    = Either Jim or Sue is absent.
6.<보기>에서 어법상 옳은 문장끼리 짝지은 것은?[간접의문문]

(a) Please tell me how is Tom.
(b) Do you think what he should do?
(c) Does she know where her friend lives?
(d) Can you tell me who the window broke?
(e) I wonder if you will come to my party.
(f) What do you believe happened?

  1. ①(a), (c), (e)
  2. ②(b), (d), (f)
  3. ③(a), (b), (e)
  4. ④(c), (d), (e)
  5. ⑤(c), (e), (f)
7.다음 <보기>에서 어법상 옳은 문장의 개수는?[간접의문문]

(a) I wonder whether Cindy likes me.
(b) Do you remember what time was it?
(c) How far do you suppose he can run?
(d) Mike doesn't know if there is a bank nearby.
(e) Please tell me what should I do.
(f) I'm not sure when he arrived.

  1. ①1개
  2. ②2개
  3. ③3개
  4. ④4개
  5. ⑤5개
8.다음 우리말을 바르게 영작한 것은?[간접의문문]

그녀가 어떻게 그 문제를 풀었는지 아무도 모른다.

  1. ①Nobody knows how did she solve the problem.
  2. ②Nobody knows how she solved the problem.
  3. ③Nobody knows how she solves the problem.
  4. ④Nobody knows how solved she the problem.
  5. ⑤How does nobody know she solved the problem?
9.다음 우리말을 바르게 영작한 것은?[간접의문문]

너는 그 강이 얼마나 긴지 아니?

  1. ①Do you know how the river is long?
  2. ②How long do you know the river is?
  3. ③Do you know how long the river is?
  4. ④Do you know how long is the river?
  5. ⑤Do you know the river how long is?
10.밑줄 친 if의 쓰임이 <보기>와 같은 것은?[간접의문문]

<보기> We'll be late if we don't hurry.

  1. ①I'm not sure if the store is open.
  2. ②Do you know if he got the message?
  3. ③She asked me if I liked jazz.
  4. ④You'll feel better if you take a rest.
  5. ⑤I wonder if the rumor is true.
11.다음 중 어법상 옳은 문장은?[간접의문문]
  1. ①Do you think which is better, this or that?
  2. ②Which do you think is better, this or that?
  3. ③Which do you think better is, this or that?
  4. ④Which do you know is better, this or that?
  5. ⑤Do you think which one better is?
12.두 문장을 한 문장으로 연결한 것 중 어색한 것은?[간접의문문]
  1. ①I asked her. + Why was she sad?
    → I asked her why she was sad.
  2. ②Tell me. + What did he say?
    → Tell me what he said.
  3. ③Do you guess? + Who called me?
    → Do you guess who called me?
  4. ④Do you think? + Why did she quit?
    → Why do you think she quit?
  5. ⑤I'd like to know. + Where did they go?
    → I'd like to know where they went.
13.우리말과 일치하도록 괄호 안의 말을 배열할 때 세 번째로 오는 단어는?[간접의문문]

그들이 그 일을 좋아하는지 넌 알고 있니?

14.다음 우리말을 영작할 때 네 번째로 오는 단어는?[간접의문문]

16.다음 대화의 빈칸에 들어갈 말로 알맞은 것은?[간접의문문]

18.다음 문장을 간접의문문으로 바꿀 때 빈칸에 알맞은 것은?[간접의문문]

19.다음 글의 밑줄 친 ⓐ~ⓔ 중 어법상 어색한 것은?[종합]

20.<보기>에서 어법상 틀린 문장을 모두 고른 것은?[간접의문문]

1 ② 이유 ▶ (A) B(the girl) is (B) 앞(The girl) is (C) both → are
포인트 ▶ not only A but also B · either A or B · neither A nor B → 동사는 B에 / B as well as A → 동사는 B(앞)에 / both A and B → 복수

2 ⑤ 이유 ▶ Not only 맨 앞 → does + 주어 + 동사원형
오답 ▶ ① 도치 안 함 ② 일반동사는 do로 도치 ③·④ does/did 뒤 원형

3 ③ 이유 ▶ 유용할 뿐만 아니라 재미있다 = fun as well as useful ✔
오답 ▶ ① 못 한다로 바뀜 ② 간호사가 아니라 가수 ④ 둘 다 싫어함 → neither milk nor juice ⑤ 병렬 ❌ → enjoys not only painting but also singing

4 ④ 이유 ▶ (a)·(d)·(f) 옳음 ((f) but also의 also는 생략 가능)
오답 ▶ (b) B(Mr. Kim) → wants (c) singing과 나란히 → dancing (e) 앞(Sora) → is
포인트 ▶ not only A but also B · either A or B · neither A nor B → 동사는 B에 / B as well as A → 동사는 B(앞)에 / both A and B → 복수

5 ① 이유 ▶ math as well as science = not only science but (also) math ✔
오답 ▶ ② as 빠짐(as well as) ③ A·B 자리가 바뀜 → Not only her mouth but also her eyes smile ④ 예쁘지 않다로 바뀜 ⑤ 둘 중 하나로 바뀜

6 ⑤ 이유 ▶ (c)·(e)·(f) 옳음
오답 ▶ (a) how Tom is (b) What do you think he should do? (d) 의문사 주어 → who broke the window
포인트 ▶ think · believe · suppose · guess · imagine 이면 의문사를 맨 앞으로: 의문사 + do you think + 주어 + 동사

7 ④ 이유 ▶ 옳은 것 (a)·(c)·(d)·(f) 4개
오답 ▶ (b) what time it was (e) what I should do
포인트 ▶ 간접의문문 = 의문사 + 주어 + 동사 (do/does/did는 빼고 시제는 동사에)

8 ② 이유 ▶ how + 주어 + 동사(과거)
오답 ▶ ① did ❌ ③ 풀었는지 → 과거 ④ 어순 ⑤ know는 의문사를 앞으로 보내지 않음

9 ③ 이유 ▶ how long(한 덩어리) + 주어 + 동사
오답 ▶ ① how long을 떼면 ❌ ② know는 의문사를 앞으로 보내지 않음 ④·⑤ 어순

10 ④ 이유 ▶ <보기>의 if = 만약 ~라면(조건). 같은 것은 쉬면 나아질 것
오답 ▶ ①·②·③·⑤ ~인지 아닌지(간접의문문)

11 ② 이유 ▶ think → 의문사 맨 앞. which가 주어라 바로 is
오답 ▶ ①·⑤ 의문사가 뒤 ③ 어순 ④ know는 의문사를 앞으로 보내지 않음 → Do you know which is better?
포인트 ▶ think · believe · suppose · guess · imagine 이면 의문사를 맨 앞으로: 의문사 + do you think + 주어 + 동사

12 ③ 이유 ▶ guess → 의문사 맨 앞: Who do you guess called me?

13 ⑤ 이유 ▶ Do you know if they like the job? → know(1) if(2) they(3)
포인트 ▶ 의문사 없는 간접의문문 = if + 주어 + 동사

14 ① 이유 ▶ Why(1) do(2) you(3) think(4) she(5) cried(6)?
포인트 ▶ think · believe · suppose · guess · imagine 이면 의문사를 맨 앞으로: 의문사 + do you think + 주어 + 동사

15 ④ 이유 ▶ not A but B도 동사는 B(I)에 → am

포인트 ▶ not only A but also B · either A or B · neither A nor B → 동사는 B에 / B as well as A → 동사는 B(앞)에 / both A and B → 복수

16 ③ 이유 ▶ 거리를 물음 → how far + 주어 + 동사
오답 ▶ ① 어순 ② how far를 떼면 ❌ ④ 어순 ⑤ does ❌

17 ① 이유 ▶ (A) 의문사 what이 주어 → 바로 happened (B) who가 주어 → do you think 뒤에 바로 will win

18 ② 이유 ▶ 의문사 없음 → if + 주어(you → she) + 동사(did 빼고 finished)
오답 ▶ ① 인칭 ③·④ 어순 ⑤ that ❌

19 ① 이유 ▶ ⓐ → what I should do (의문사 + 주어 + 동사)
오답 ▶ ⓑ 어순 ✔ ⓒ·ⓓ B(my dad) → wants ✔ ⓔ think → 의문사 맨 앞 ✔
