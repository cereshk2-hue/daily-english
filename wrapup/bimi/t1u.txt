 audition, excited
I glanced at the clock on the wall. 10:00. That meant the casting director would be calling shortly with the results of my first audition for a musical part in The Wizard of Oz. I felt shaky all over, chewing my thumbnail and jiggling my feet. Finally, the telephone rang. While I was coming round, Dad answered. I heard him say, “Ahh, thank you. I’ll let her know...” As I got to the bottom of the stairs, he was just hanging up the phone. “That was The Wizard of Oz. You’re second senior munchkin,” he announced. I had a wave of excitement wash over me, knowing I was in — that whatever happened I could be involved in one of the productions.
