범위: 8과 간접의문문 — 의문사 + 주어 + 동사(do/does/did 빼고 시제는 동사에), 의문사가 주어면 의문사 + 동사, 의문사 없으면 if/whether, think·believe·guess·suppose·imagine이면 의문사를 맨 앞으로. 쉬운 문제부터 어려운 문제까지 고루 섞었어요.

1.다음 대화의 빈칸에 들어갈 말로 알맞은 것은?[의문사 + 주어 + 동사 · 하]

A: Do you know Nick arrives?
B: He arrives at 7 p.m.

  1. ①when
  2. ②what
  3. ③who
  4. ④which
  5. ⑤whose
2.두 문장을 한 문장으로 바르게 연결한 것은?[의문사 + 주어 + 동사 · 하]

Do you know? + Why did he run?

  1. ①Do you know why did he run?
  2. ②Do you know why he did run?
  3. ③Do you know why he runs?
  4. ④Do you know why does he run?
  5. ⑤Do you know why he ran?
3.두 문장을 한 문장으로 바르게 연결한 것은?[의문사 + 주어 + 동사 · 하]

I don't know. + When will he be home for supper?

  1. ①I don't know when will he be home for supper.
  2. ②I don't know when he be home for supper.
  3. ③I don't know that when he will be home for supper.
  4. ④I don't know when he will be home for supper.
  5. ⑤I don't know when be he will home for supper.
4.다음 빈칸에 들어갈 말로 알맞은 것은?[if / whether · 하]

I don't know. + Is he diligent or lazy?
→ I don't know he is diligent or lazy.

  1. ①what
  2. ②where
  3. ③who
  4. ④whether
  5. ⑤when
5.다음 우리말을 영어로 바르게 옮긴 것은?[의문사 + 주어 + 동사 · 하]

나는 그녀가 어디에 사는지 모른다.

  1. ①I don't know where she lives.
  2. ②I don't know where does she live.
  3. ③I don't know where she live.
  4. ④I don't know she lives where.
  5. ⑤I don't know where lives she.
6.두 문장을 한 문장으로 바르게 연결한 것은?[if / whether · 하]

I'm not sure. + Can she swim?

  1. ①I'm not sure can she swim.
  2. ②I'm not sure if can she swim.
  3. ③I'm not sure that can she swim.
  4. ④I'm not sure what she can swim.
  5. ⑤I'm not sure if she can swim.
7.다음 빈칸에 들어갈 말로 알맞은 것은?[의문사 + 주어 + 동사 · 중]

  1. ①what
  2. ②how
  3. ③who
  4. ④when
  5. ⑤where

12.다음 문장을 간접의문문을 써서 바르게 바꾼 것은?[화법 · 쓰임 구별 · 중]

  4. ④I asked her how she was Pete's sister.

13.다음 중 어법상 바르지 않은 것은?[간접의문문 판단 · 중]
  1. ①You must tell me what did he tell you.

  4. ④Nobody knows what the answer is.

14.다음 중 어법상 올바른 문장은?[간접의문문 판단 · 중]
  1. ①Nobody knows when did the war break out.

15.다음 우리말을 영어로 바르게 옮긴 것은?[think류 → 의문사 맨 앞 · 상]

  1. ①Do you think who will win the game tomorrow?
  2. ②Do you think who will the game win tomorrow?
  3. ③Who do you think will win the game tomorrow?
  4. ④Who do you think the game will win tomorrow?
  5. ⑤Who will win do you think the game tomorrow?
16.다음 문장을 간접의문문으로 바꾼 문장에서 밑줄 친 부분 중 어색한 것은?[화법 · 쓰임 구별 · 상]

→ Tom ①asked me ②if ③he ④was done with ⑤my homework.

17.다음 빈칸에 들어갈 수 있는 것은 모두 몇 개인가?[간접의문문 판단 · 상]

Can you tell me ?

(a) what is the truth
(b) who owns this house
(c) if the story was true
(d) what does she look like
(e) how long were you here

  1. ①1개
  2. ②2개
  3. ③3개
  4. ④4개
  5. ⑤5개
18.다음 중 어법상 틀린 문장끼리 짝지은 것은?[간접의문문 판단 · 상]

(a) Do you know who stole her bag?
(b) Do you know where she went last night?
(c) I don't know where is the flea market.
(d) I don't know what should I do.
(e) I wonder if he was smart and diligent.

  1. ①(c), (d)
  2. ②(a), (c)
  3. ③(b), (d)
  4. ④(b), (e)
  5. ⑤(d), (e)
19.다음 문장을 간접의문문을 써서 바르게 바꾼 것은?[화법 · 쓰임 구별 · 상]

  5. ⑤She asked me that I lived where.
20.두 문장을 한 문장으로 바르게 연결한 것은?[think류 → 의문사 맨 앞 · 상]

  1. ①What do you imagine Melissa say to me?
  2. ②What did you imagine Melissa said to me?
  3. ③Do you imagine what Melissa said to me?
  4. ④What do you imagine did Melissa say to me?
  5. ⑤What do you imagine Melissa said to me?
