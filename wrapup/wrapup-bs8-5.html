범위: 8과 문법 — not only A but also B(= B as well as A, 수 일치·병렬), 간접의문문(의문사+주어+동사, if/whether, think류 동사). 교재 문제를 바탕으로 문장을 바꾸고 더 어렵게 만들었어요. 문장을 끝까지 읽고 고르세요.

1.빈칸에 들어갈 말을 순서대로 바르게 짝지은 것은?[not only ~ but also]

• Either Ben you have the key.
• Both Sujin and Mina planning a trip.
• Jake as well as I with the plan.

  1. ①or − are − agrees
  2. ②nor − is − agree
  3. ③nor − are − agree
  4. ④or − are − agree
  5. ⑤or − is − agrees
2.다음 <보기>에서 어법상 옳은 문장의 개수는?[not only ~ but also]

(a) She enjoys not only singing but also to dance.
(b) Not only did she dance, but she also sings last night.
(c) His words as well as his behavior upset me yesterday.
(d) Not only Jane but also Eric likes math.
(e) Not only the students but also the teacher were glad.
(f) She as well as her friends have the same problem.

  1. ①1개
  2. ②2개
  3. ③3개
  4. ④4개
  5. ⑤5개
3.다음 중 어법상 옳은 문장은?[not only ~ but also]
  1. ①Not only he is a singer but also an actor.
  2. ②Not only you but also she are pretty.
  3. ③I'm interested not only in art but in history.
  4. ④The place is not only wonderful but also a good one to visit.
  5. ⑤Not only she can sing, but she also can dance.
4.다음 문장과 의미가 같은 것은?[not only ~ but also]

Mina not only sings well but also plays the piano well.

  1. ①Mina just plays the piano well.
  2. ②Mina doesn't sing well but plays the piano well.
  3. ③Mina plays the piano well as well as sings well.
  4. ④Mina sings well because she plays the piano well.
  5. ⑤Mina neither sings nor plays the piano well.
5.두 문장을 한 문장으로 바르게 만든 것은?[not only ~ but also]
  1. ①Ann is a teacher. She is also a writer.
    → Ann is not a teacher but a writer.
  2. ②Ted doesn't eat meat. He doesn't eat fish either.
    → Ted eats not only meat but also fish.
  3. ③The cook enjoys baking. He also enjoys singing.
    → The cook not only enjoys baking but also singing.
  4. ④My shoes are old. My bag is old, too.
    → Not only my shoes but also my bag are old.
  5. ⑤Leo has been to Japan. Kyle has been to Japan, too.
    → Not only Leo but also Kyle has been to Japan.

7.다음 중 어법상 어색한 문장은?[간접의문문]

  2. ②I don't know where this bus goes.

  5. ⑤Do you know when the shop closes?
8.두 문장을 한 문장으로 연결한 것 중 어색한 것은?[간접의문문]
  1. ①I'd like to know. + Where did you go?
    → I'd like to know where you go.
  2. ②I need to know. + Where did you go last night?
    → I need to know where you went last night.
  3. ③Please tell me. + How long do I have to wait?
    → Please tell me how long I have to wait.
  4. ④I wonder. + Who ate my cookies?
    → I wonder who ate my cookies.
  5. ⑤Can I ask you? + How much is it?
    → Can I ask you how much it is?
9.밑줄 친 부분 중 어법상 옳은 것은?[간접의문문]
  1. ①I asked him what is his job.
  2. ②I wonder if he will comes.
  3. ③No one knows who made this robot.
  4. ④I don't know how tall is he.
  5. ⑤Do you know what she has a dog?
10.짝지어진 두 문장의 의미가 서로 다른 것은?[not only ~ but also]
  1. ①We may meet today. Or we may meet tomorrow. We don't know yet.
    = We may meet not only today but also tomorrow.
  2. ②Not only Brielle's heart rate but also her temperature came back to normal.
    = Her temperature as well as her heart rate came back to normal.
  3. ③He is strong, and he is fast, too.
    = He is not only strong but also fast.
  4. ④Mina reads comics, and her sister does, too.
    = Not only Mina but also her sister reads comics.
  5. ⑤Kids as well as adults love this game.
    = Not only adults but also kids love this game.

1 ① 이유 ▶ either A or B / both → are / B as well as A → 앞(Jake) agrees
포인트 ▶ not only A but also B · either A or B · neither A nor B → 동사는 B에 / B as well as A → 동사는 B(앞)에 / both A and B → 복수

2 ② 이유 ▶ 옳은 것 (c)·(d) 2개
오답 ▶ (a) → dancing (b) last night → sang (e) B(the teacher) → was (f) 앞(She) → has
포인트 ▶ not only A but also B · either A or B · neither A nor B → 동사는 B에 / B as well as A → 동사는 B(앞)에 / both A and B → 복수

3 ③ 이유 ▶ 전치사구끼리(in art / in history) ✔, also 생략 가능
오답 ▶ ① 도치 → Not only is he a singer, but he is also an actor. ② B(she) → is ④ 형용사끼리 → good to visit ⑤ 도치 → Not only can she sing

4 ③ 이유 ▶ not only A but also B = B as well as A
오답 ▶ ① 피아노만 ② 노래를 못함 ④ 이유로 바뀜 ⑤ 둘 다 못함

5 ⑤ 이유 ▶ B(Kyle) → has ✔
오답 ▶ ① 교사가 아니라 작가 ② 둘 다 안 먹음 → neither ③ 병렬 ❌ → enjoys not only baking but also singing ④ B(my bag) → is

6 ② 이유 ▶ which color + 주어 + 동사 ✔
오답 ▶ ① How far do you suppose he can run? ③ last night → made ④ how hard it is ⑤ Which one do you think is smarter?
포인트 ▶ think · believe · suppose · guess · imagine 이면 의문사를 맨 앞으로: 의문사 + do you think + 주어 + 동사

7 ④ 이유 ▶ does를 빼고 he keeps → Where do you think he keeps the file?
오답 ▶ ①·③ 의문사 맨 앞 + 주어 + 동사 ✔ ②·⑤ 의문사 + 주어 + 동사 ✔
포인트 ▶ think · believe · suppose · guess · imagine 이면 의문사를 맨 앞으로: 의문사 + do you think + 주어 + 동사

8 ① 이유 ▶ did를 빼면 시제는 동사로: where you went (go ❌)
오답 ▶ ②·③·④·⑤ 바르게 연결

9 ③ 이유 ▶ 의문사 who가 주어 → 바로 made ✔
오답 ▶ ① what his job was ② will 뒤 원형 come ④ how tall he is ⑤ if(whether) she has a dog

10 ① 이유 ▶ 오늘 또는 내일(둘 중 하나) → either today or tomorrow. not only ~ but also는 둘 다
오답 ▶ ②·③·④·⑤ 같은 뜻

11 ② 이유 ▶ ⓐ·ⓒ(직접의문문)·ⓓ(의문사 없는 Do you think ~?)·ⓕ 옳음
오답 ▶ ⓑ she → wants ⓔ What do you think I can do ~? ⓖ how much the bag is
포인트 ▶ think · believe · suppose · guess · imagine 이면 의문사를 맨 앞으로: 의문사 + do you think + 주어 + 동사

12 ④ 이유 ▶ 틀린 것 ⓑ·ⓒ·ⓓ·ⓔ 4개: how much one ticket is / Who do you think will win? / how long giraffes live / when you saw him
포인트 ▶ 간접의문문 = 의문사 + 주어 + 동사 (do/does/did는 빼고 시제는 동사에)

13 ④ 이유 ▶ believe → 의문사 맨 앞 + 주어 + 동사(과거 lost)
오답 ▶ ①·⑤ 의문사가 뒤 ② did 그대로 ③ 시제
포인트 ▶ think · believe · suppose · guess · imagine 이면 의문사를 맨 앞으로: 의문사 + do you think + 주어 + 동사

14 ⑤ 이유 ▶ 문제는 ~라는 것이다(보어) → that
오답 ▶ ①·②·③·④ ~인지 아닌지 → if

15 ③ 이유 ▶ B(my brother, 단수) → doesn't ✔
오답 ▶ ① → doesn't ② 앞(My brother) → doesn't ④ B(my brother) → likes ⑤ B(my brother) → doesn't
포인트 ▶ not only A but also B · either A or B · neither A nor B → 동사는 B에 / B as well as A → 동사는 B(앞)에 / both A and B → 복수

16 ② 이유 ▶ 의문사가 주어면 3인칭 단수: (A) what makes (B) 이미 먹었으니 과거 ate (who eat ❌)

17 ⑤ 이유 ▶ ⓔ → how much it cost (did를 빼고 과거 cost)
오답 ▶ ⓑ B(her friends) → were ✔ ⓒ·ⓓ 어순 ✔

18 ⑤ 이유 ▶ think → 의문사 맨 앞 + 주어 + 동사 + from
오답 ▶ ①·③ 의문사가 뒤 ②·④ 어순
포인트 ▶ think · believe · suppose · guess · imagine 이면 의문사를 맨 앞으로: 의문사 + do you think + 주어 + 동사

19 ④ 이유 ▶ 음악뿐만 아니라(A) 미술에도(B) → not only in music but also in art
오답 ▶ ① 음악이 아니라 미술 ② 둘 다 아님 ③ B as well as A → '미술뿐만 아니라 음악에도'(A·B 바뀜) ⑤ 둘 중 하나

20 ① 이유 ▶ who those people are (who가 보어)
오답 ▶ ② B(I) am ✔ ③ whether + 주어 + 동사 ✔ ④ 의문사 주어 + think ✔ ⑤ 형용사끼리 ✔
포인트 ▶ 간접의문문 = 의문사 + 주어 + 동사 (do/does/did는 빼고 시제는 동사에)
