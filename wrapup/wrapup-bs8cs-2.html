범위: 8과 간접의문문 — 의문사 + 주어 + 동사(do/does/did 빼고 시제는 동사에), 의문사가 주어면 의문사 + 동사, 의문사 없으면 if/whether, think·believe·guess·suppose·imagine이면 의문사를 맨 앞으로. 쉬운 문제부터 어려운 문제까지 고루 섞었어요.

1.다음 빈칸에 들어갈 말로 알맞은 것은?[if / whether · 하]

  1. ①who
  2. ②which
  3. ③whether
  4. ④what
  5. ⑤whose
2.다음 우리말을 영어로 바르게 옮긴 것은?[의문사 + 주어 + 동사 · 하]

  2. ②Do you know how old is he?
  3. ③Do you know he is old?
  4. ④How old do you know is he?
  5. ⑤How old do you know he is?
3.다음 우리말을 영어로 바르게 옮긴 것은?[의문사 + 주어 + 동사 · 하]

  1. ①I wonder I should read which book.
  2. ②I wonder which book I should read.
  3. ③I wonder which book should I read.
  4. ④Which book I wonder should I read.
  5. ⑤Which book I wonder I should read.

17.두 문장을 한 문장으로 바르게 연결한 것끼리 짝지은 것은?[간접의문문 판단 · 상]

(a) Tell me. + What did you buy? → Tell me what you bought.
(b) I wonder. + What made your parents upset? → I wonder what your parents made upset.
(c) I know. + What did you do after school yesterday? → I know what you did after school yesterday.
(d) Do you think? + Why did he cut the class? → Why did you think he cut the class?
(e) We asked her, "Are you from America?" → We asked her you are from America or not.

  1. ①(a), (c)
  2. ②(a), (b)
  3. ③(b), (d)
  4. ④(c), (e)
  5. ⑤(d), (e)
18.다음 중 어법상 옳은 문장끼리 짝지은 것은?[think류 → 의문사 맨 앞 · 상]

(a) When do you think Howard will go abroad?
(b) Where do you think Gangnam Station is?
(c) Do you think who will win the first prize?
(d) Do you believe who is rich?
(e) Do you think what is the best food?

  1. ①(a), (c)
  2. ②(b), (d)
  3. ③(c), (e)
  4. ④(d), (e)
  5. ⑤(a), (b)
19.다음 대화의 밑줄 친 ⓐ~ⓔ 중 어법상 틀린 것은?[think류 → 의문사 맨 앞 · 상]

  4. ④ⓓ
  5. ⑤ⓔ
