Before the lesson with Mrs. Jones, Ann was watching Victoria’s flawless technique on her smartphone, and it made her chest feel tighter. What was the point? Ann thought. I’ll never be like her. As the lesson began ,her teacher, Mrs. Jones, immediately noticed Ann’s distraction. “Is everything alright, Ann?” asked Mrs. Jones. “I’m just wondering if I’m good enough to be special like Victoria,” Ann said. “I used to love playing. Now I just feel like I’m not good enough. I don’t have the perfect technique like her.” Hearing her words, Mrs. Jones smiled softly and said, “That’s because you’re measuring yourself against someone else’s ruler. Comparison steals your joy and unique voice. Stop looking at her journey and focus on your own.” Hearing those comforting words, something profound shifted inside Ann. After the lesson, Ann took down Victoria Gray’s picture that had always been on her bedroom wall. That night, Ann deleted her social media app from her smartphone. She focused on practicing her violin, trying to find herself, for a week. On Saturday, Ann picked up her violin and played a simple piece she’d known since childhood. Her music had mistakes, but it felt like her own music again. When she finished, her little sister Emma clapped enthusiastically. “When you play, it makes the whole house feel nicer.” Ann smiled, finally at peace.
① We should constantly compare our abilities with those of talented people to improve our performance.
② True happiness comes from achieving perfection and receiving recognition from others.
③ We should focus on our own growth and appreciate our unique abilities rather than comparing ourselves with others.
④ Following successful people's paths is the most reliable way to discover our hidden talents.
⑤ Social media is essential for developing confidence and maintaining motivation in pursuing our goals.
